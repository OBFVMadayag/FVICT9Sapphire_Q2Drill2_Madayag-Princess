A DIAMOND LEGACY OF LEARNING

At OBMC, every child follows their journey in climbing up to the holistic method of Dr. Maria Montessori.

Welcome to OBMontessori Center, the pioneer of Montessori education in the Philippines!

OBMontessori Center is a pre-k to 12 co-educational school, offering a variety of alternatives to every international school. At OBMC, a child's tradition and innovation has been blended to prepare for their school life.


Our mission is to let our students know how they transition into self developing and also let our individuals know who can succeed, direct, and build the future of the changing world.


Have a deep exploration on our programs and have a look at the vibrant community that makes OBMC a unique and special place for child development.

Campuses


OBMC Angeles Campus


OBMC Angeles Campus, located in Pampanga, provides a nurturing and dynamic environment for students to thrive.

OBMC Farview Campus


OBMC Farview Campus, located in Quezon City, offers quality Montessori education to its thriving community to families.

OBMC Sta. Ana Campus


OBMC Sta. Ana Campus, located in the heritage district of Manila, is known for strong academic programs, community involvement, and love for culture and heritage.


OBMC Greenhills Campus


OBMC Greenhills Campus, located in San Juan City, offers a very creative and fun environment for students to spark imagination, learning, and service.

OBMC Las Pinas Campus


OBMC Las Pinas Campus, located in Las Pinas City, pays a homage to the area's culture, birthplace, and the unique bamboo organ. Its atmosphere promotes the development and welcoming of students.
